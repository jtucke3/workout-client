Set up 2FA

This is where your authenticator setup flow will go (QR code, secret key, and test code).

  1. Open your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.).
  2. Add a new account and scan the QR code from this screen.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code from your app to confirm.
